Our story

Food made with
heart & honesty

Al-Halal Restaurant was founded on a simple belief: that food shared with loved ones should always be wholesome, delicious, and trustworthy.

Every dish on our menu is prepared with 100% halal-certified ingredients, fresh produce, and recipes passed down through generations. We bring the flavours of East Africa and the Mediterranean together under one roof.
